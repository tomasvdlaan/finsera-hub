A meeting type (a "template") gives a new note an agenda and a skeleton of headings, so you do not start from a blank page. You choose a type when you [start a meeting](help:meetings/start-a-meeting); you cannot change a note's type afterwards. Every note starts with the Finsera letterhead image and a rule, because notes are often mailed or printed.

## The types

| Type | Button | Timebox | Agenda it starts with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily stand-up | **Daily stand-up** | 15 min | Round the table; Blockers; Anything that changes the sprint goal |
| Sprint planning | **Sprint planning** | 60 min | What is the goal; Capacity and dates; What comes in; What we are deliberately not doing; What we depend on; Risks |
| Sprint review | **Sprint review** | 45 min | What we finished; What we did not, and why; What we showed; Feedback; What that changes |
| Retrospective | **Retrospective** | 45 min | How the last actions went; What went well; What did not; What we still do not understand; What we change next |
| Client check-in | **Client check-in** | 30 min | What we said we would do last time; How the current work is landing; Blockers on their side; What is next; Anything commercial |
| Project kick-off | **Project kick-off** | 60 min | Why this project exists; What are we actually delivering; What we are not delivering; Who does what; How we will work together; Data and access we need; Dates and milestones; Risks and unknowns |
| Discovery / intake | **Discovery / intake** | 60 min | What problem are they trying to solve; How it is handled today; What they have tried; Where the data lives; Who decides; Budget and timing; Whether we are a fit |

The first four are the sprint ceremonies and appear as one-click buttons in the **Start a room** bar. The other three appear in the **Name it first** form.

A **Blank meeting** or a **Just a note** has no type, no agenda and an empty body.

## What gets prefilled

- **Stand-up:** a block per attendee ("Yesterday", "Today", "In flight"), filled in from what that person moved on the board since the previous stand-up on the same project (or since yesterday if it is the first). **Today** is always left blank. The sprint goal and any blocked cards are filled in too. If there is no project, or the board cannot be read, you get the empty headings instead.
- **Sprint review:** the cards that were finished and not finished in the sprint, the sprint goal, and the board's definition of done.
- **Retrospective:** a **Last time we said we would** list of the previous retrospective's actions, ticked if done.
- **Any note with a project or client:** commitments left open by earlier meetings about the same project (or client, if there is no project) are added under **Since last time**, and the agenda gets "What we said we would do" as its first item. A retrospective skips this, because it already opens with its own list.

All of this happens once, when the note is created. After that it is ordinary text you can edit.

## Timebox

The timebox is advisory. In the [room](help:meetings/run-the-room) the clock shows elapsed time against it and turns to "over" when you pass it; on the note page a ceremony that overran says "N m over". Nothing stops the meeting or the recording at the limit.

## The assistant's starting settings

Each type also sets how forward the [meeting assistant](help:meetings/meeting-assistant) starts: for example a stand-up keeps note-taking reserved, a client check-in writes notes generously but stays quiet in conversation, and a kick-off is eager about notes and action points. You can change these per note.

## Good to know

- Agenda items are plain rows you can add to and remove from on the note afterwards. See [Agenda and people](help:meetings/agenda-and-people).
- A note created with an explicit agenda or body (for example by the assistant) is not seeded with the "Since last time" block.
