**Money → Contracts** is the register of what has been agreed with clients and when it lapses.

## Steps

1. Open **Money** and choose the **Contracts** tab.
2. If any signed contract needs attention, a red line says how many ("N contracts need attention — see the badges below").
3. Use the **Filter type** dropdown: **All types** or one of **Raamovereenkomst**, **Opdrachtbevestiging**, **NDA**, **Verwerkersovereenkomst**, **Overig**.
4. Click a title to open [the contract page](help:sales/contract-page).

To record a new contract use the form above the list: [Add a contract](help:sales/add-contract).

## What each row shows

Title, type badge, status (**draft**, **signed**, **terminated**), an urgency badge, client name, "ends <date>" and "auto-renews". The urgency badge is shown for **signed** contracts only and is the first that applies:

1. **expired**: the end date has passed (red).
2. **notice by <date>**: the last day to give notice is within the next 30 days (red).
3. **ends in N days**: the contract ends within 60 days (not red).

## Good to know

- All of these are worked out from today's date. Nothing changes status by itself; an expired contract stays **signed** until someone terminates it.
- The notice deadline is the end date minus the notice period in days.
- There is no status filter on the screen, only type.
