The Finsera platform is the business's own working tool: clients and projects, hours, documents, the board, quotes and invoices, meetings, and the client portal all live in one place, and the assistant can answer questions across all of it.

## How the screen is laid out

- **The top bar.** The platform has a bar across the top rather than a side rail. On the left is the Finsera name. In the middle is a row of section links: **Today**, **Inbox**, **Agenda**, **Board**, **Time**, **Clients**, **Money**, **Meetings** and **Docs**. On the right are the clock (**Start a timer**, or the running timer), the search button (**⌘K**), the assistant button (✦) and your avatar. Details are in [The top bar and moving around](help:shell/navigation).
- **Your avatar menu.** The round letter at the far right opens a menu with your name and role, **Organisation**, **People**, **Platform costs** (administrators), **Platform modules**, and **Sign out**.
- **The command bar.** Press **⌘K** (or **Ctrl+K**) anywhere to jump to a page, find a record by name, start a stand-up, or ask the assistant a question. See [The command bar](help:shell/command-bar).
- **Section tabs.** Some pages are not in the top bar at all. They are reached from a hub page that shows a strip of tabs, for example [All work](help:shell/work-hub) and [Money](help:billing/money-hub).

## Finding things

1. Press **⌘K** and type part of a name. It finds clients, projects, tasks, documents, invoices and meeting notes, whichever module they belong to.
2. Or browse by section: the top bar for the nine main places, a hub's tabs for the rest.
3. Or ask. The assistant at **✦** answers questions about your clients, hours, invoices and meetings, and it can also answer "how do I…" questions from Help. See [The assistant](help:shell/assistant).
4. Or open [Help](help:help/index) and search it.

## Good to know

- You only ever see what your role allows. If a page, button or tab described in Help is missing for you, it is almost always a permission. See [Roles and permissions](help:shell/roles-and-permissions).
- The assistant acts as you, with your permissions, and nothing more.
- Help itself is described in [Help: search, browse and reference](help:help/index).
