The **Agenda** collects every dated thing in the platform into one calendar: when the meetings are, which sprint you are in, which cards, action points, invoices and quotes fall due, and the hours you have logged. It only displays; there is nothing to create here.

## What you see

- **Header:** the period in words, **←** (**Previous**), **Today**, **→** (**Next**), and three tabs: **Week**, **Day** and **List**.
- **Left (wide):** the calendar.
- **Right:** **Closing in**, a list of what is coming due.

### The three views
- **Week** and **Day** show an hour grid. Meetings that were recorded and hours you logged appear as blocks at their time. A line marks the current time.
  - Sprints appear as a band above the grid labelled **In flight**.
  - Things with a date but no time (a card due, an action point, an invoice falling due, a quote running out) appear as dots under the day's name.
- **List** looks a fortnight ahead from the chosen date, one section per day, and leaves out empty days.
- **←**/**→** step by a week, a day or a fortnight depending on the view. **Today** jumps back.

### What each thing means
- **Meetings** - recorded meetings sit at their time; a note written up afterwards is only a dot on its day. Select to open.
- **Sprints** - the planned dates of each sprint.
- **Cards** (due dates) - only open cards. Finished ones drop out.
- **Action points** - from meetings, with the meeting's name.
- **Invoices** - issued and draft invoices by due date, with the amount. Paid and void invoices drop out. A draft is never treated as late, because nobody has been asked to pay it yet.
- **Quotes** - sent quotes by the date they stop being valid.
- **Logged hours** - your own hours with a start and an end time, drawn behind the plan. Entries recorded only as a number of minutes have no place on a clock and are left out.

### Closing in
The side list shows cards, action points, invoices and quotes due within the next two weeks (and anything already past), most urgent first (at most twelve), with "today", "tomorrow", "in 3 days" or "2 days ago". Its subtitle says how many are already past, or **Nothing is late**. Colours: red is past due, amber is due today.

## Good to know

- **The view and date are in the address**, so you can send a colleague a link to a particular week.
- **It is not a personal calendar.** Cards, action points, invoices and meetings are the business's, not only yours.
- **If a source fails to load**, a box called **Not everything loaded** names which one (for example Invoices) rather than silently drawing an incomplete calendar.
- External calendars such as Outlook are not shown here.
