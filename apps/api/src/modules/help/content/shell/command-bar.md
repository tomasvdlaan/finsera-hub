One box that goes anywhere, finds anything with a name, and answers questions. It works on every page, including inside a meeting room.

## Open it

- Press **⌘K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows), or select the search button (**⌕**) in the top bar.
- Press **Esc**, or click outside the box, to close it. Pressing the shortcut again also closes it.

## What it offers

With nothing typed, it shows the **last five records you opened from here**, then the main pages from the top bar and the hubs.

Once you type, the list shows, in this order:

1. **Actions** whose names match what you typed (see below).
2. **Pages** whose names match, up to five (for example type "ti" for **Timesheet**).
3. **Records** whose *name* contains what you typed, from every module: clients, projects, tasks, sprints, documents, meeting notes, invoices, quotes, contracts and so on. The label beside each says what kind it is. You need at least **two characters**, and up to 20 matches are returned. Names that start with what you typed come first.
4. **Ask the assistant - "<what you typed>"**, always last.

## Steps

1. Open the bar and type.
2. Move with **↑** and **↓**, open with **↵** (Enter), or just click a row.
3. Pick a page or record to go there. Records you open this way are remembered for next time.

### Actions
The bar can start two things, which would otherwise need a trip to the Meetings page:
- **Start a daily stand-up** creates a stand-up note for today with you as attendee and takes you straight into its meeting room.
- **New meeting note** creates an "Untitled meeting" note and opens it.

### Ask the assistant
1. Type your question and choose the last row, or move to it and press Enter.
2. The box turns into a conversation. The reply appears below; type follow-ups in the **Reply…** box.
3. Select **Open in full →** to continue on the [assistant page](help:shell/assistant) with the whole conversation carried over.

If you were looking at a record (its address contains the record's id), the assistant is told which one, so "this client" works.

## Good to know

- **It searches names, not contents.** To find what a document or meeting says, ask the assistant, or search inside [Documents](help:docs/index) or [Meetings](help:meetings/index).
- **You only find what you may see.** Records of a kind you are not allowed to read are not searched at all.
- Pages that live behind a hub (such as the Money sub-pages) are not listed as pages; use the hub, or search for the record by name. A page can be listed even if your role does not allow you to use it; it will then refuse you.
- The five recent records are stored in this browser only; a different browser starts empty.
- If an action fails, the reason is shown in red at the bottom of the box.
- All keys are in [Keyboard shortcuts](help:shell/keyboard-shortcuts).
