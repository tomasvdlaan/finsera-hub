Often what you want to keep is one answer, not a whole conversation: the paragraph that finally explained a rule, in a thread that was mostly about something else. **Saved answers** collects those.

## Steps

### Star an answer
1. Under a finished answer in the assistant, select **star**. It reads **unstar** once starred.

### Find your saved answers
1. In the assistant's conversation list, select the star icon (**Saved answers**).
2. Each saved answer is shown in full. Above it is the title of its conversation: select that to go back to the thread it came from.

### Remove one
1. On **Saved answers**, select **unstar** next to it. Or select **unstar** under it in the conversation.

## Good to know

- Nothing saved yet shows "Nothing saved yet. Star an answer and it appears here, with a way back to the conversation it came from."
- **star** only appears on an answer that has been saved in the conversation. If you do not see it on an answer that has only just arrived, reopen the conversation from the list.
- A saved answer is a copy of the text at that moment; open the cited records for the current facts.
- **← Assistant** at the top of the page takes you back.
