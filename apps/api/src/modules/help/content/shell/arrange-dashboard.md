Every colleague has their own dashboard. Changes save as you make them; there is no Save button.

## Steps

### Start editing
1. Open **Today** and select **Edit** (top right). The page now says "Drag to reorder, ⚙ to configure, ✕ to remove. Every change saves as you make it."
2. Select **Done** when finished.

### Add a widget
1. In editing mode, select **Add a widget**. The **Widget library** opens as a panel.
2. Pick one. Each entry shows its name and one line saying what it tells you.
3. It is added at the end at its usual width. Select **Done**, or keep editing.

You may place the same widget more than once, for example pointed at two different projects.

### Reorder
Drag a widget by its body to a new place. Dragging also works from the keyboard: focus a widget, press space, move with the arrow keys, press space again.

### Resize
Select the small `4/12`-style button on the widget. Each click cycles to the next width: 3, 4, 6, 8, 9 or 12 columns out of twelve (never narrower than the widget's own minimum), then back round.

### Configure a widget
1. Select **⚙** on a widget that has it. A panel opens named after the widget.
2. Change the setting. Depending on the widget this is a choice, a project, a client, or a number within a limit.
3. It applies at once. Select **Done** or press Esc.

A freshly added widget that needs a project or client shows **Not chosen yet** until you pick one.

### Remove a widget
1. Select **✕** on the widget.
2. A message says "<widget> removed" with an **Undo**. Undo puts it back in its old place, with its settings.

### Reset
1. In editing mode, select **Reset**. It is shown only once you have made changes.
2. Your layout is discarded and the starter layout returns. This replaces your whole arrangement and has no undo.

## Good to know

- A dashboard holds at most 40 widgets.
- If a change cannot be saved you see "That change could not be saved".
- An empty dashboard shows "This dashboard is empty" with an **Add a widget** link.
- Widgets keep showing their real content while you edit, so you can judge the layout as you go.
