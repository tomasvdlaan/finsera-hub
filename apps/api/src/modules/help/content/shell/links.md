A link says that one record is connected to another, so a person looking at either can find the other. Links appear under a **Links** heading on cards, projects, clients and sprints.

## What you see

Each link shows a small badge with its kind, the name of the other record (select it to open), and **remove** where removal is allowed. With none, the section says "No links yet."

## Steps

### Add a link
1. In the **Links** section, open the **Link to…** list and choose a record. The list offers the records that page supplies (for example, on a client, other clients and all projects).
2. Choose the kind: **about**, **discussed**, **originated_from** or **relates_to**.
3. Select **Link**. The new link appears, and the page's history updates.

### Remove a link
1. Select **remove** next to it.

## Good to know

- **Some links have no remove button.** Those are structural: a required link, for example a card to its project, is part of what the record is and cannot be removed.
- You can link only records you are allowed to see, and a record cannot be linked to itself. Making the same link twice does nothing.
- A link is shown on both records, whichever end you made it from.
- Adding or removing a link shows up in the record's **Timeline**; see [Activity and history](help:shell/activity-timeline).
