While a live meeting is being recorded, a bar appears at the top of the page content so you cannot forget that it is running. How to run a meeting is in [Meetings](help:meetings/index).

## What it shows

**Recording**, the elapsed time as mm:ss, the number of segments transcribed so far, and the running cost in euros, and a link **Back to the meeting**.

## Steps

### Return to the meeting
1. Select **Back to the meeting**. You go to the meeting note.

### Stop it
The bar deliberately has no stop button. Stopping writes the note and ends the recording, so you do that from the meeting itself, after **Back to the meeting**.

## Good to know

- **Leaving the page does not end the meeting.** The recording carries on while you work elsewhere in the platform. That is useful, but it also means a forgotten recording keeps being transcribed, which costs money whether anyone is talking or not. The bar is there to remind you.
- If you reload or open the platform in a fresh tab while a meeting is running, it picks the meeting up again.
- The bar is not shown inside the meeting room itself, which fills the whole window.
- **⌘K** works from inside the meeting room too, to look something up without losing your place; see [The command bar](help:shell/command-bar).
