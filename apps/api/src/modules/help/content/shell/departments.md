A department is a label like Finance or Sales. It decides who work is **sent to**: an overdue invoice goes to Finance, an unanswered quote to Sales. It grants nothing; what somebody may open is still their role.

## Steps

### Put someone in a department
1. Open [People](help:shell/people).
2. In their **Departments** cell, open the list and tick the departments.

Or open their [own page](help:shell/person-page) and tick the **Departments** chips. People in no department show "In geen enkele afdeling - er wordt niets naar deze persoon gerouteerd": nothing is routed to them.

### Add a department
1. On **People**, in the **Departments** card, type a name into **Add a department**.
2. Select **Add**.

### Rename a department
1. Edit its name in the list and click away. It saves when you leave the box.

### Remove a department
1. Select **Remove** next to a custom department.

## Good to know

- Departments marked **built in** cannot be removed, because the Inbox rules address them by name.
- Anything addressed to a department that nobody belongs to reaches the administrators, so nothing goes unseen.
- Everybody can read the list of departments (so you can see that something reached you as "Finance"); only administrators can change it.
- Where the Inbox rules send things is explained in [Inbox](help:insights/index).
