Besides the business modules (clients, time, documents, board, money, meetings, portal), the platform has a set of pages and tools that belong to nobody in particular and are always there. These are what Help calls the shell.

## Good to know

- **Money** is a hub page over invoices, quotes, contracts and reporting, not a module of its own. It is explained in [The Money hub](help:billing/money-hub).
- Almost everything in the shell works on whatever your role allows. Three settings areas, **People**, **Platform costs** and the model choice, are for administrators.
