Comments are a short thread on a record, for the things that would otherwise vanish into chat: a decision, a blocker, why something changed. They sit under a **Discussion** heading on a card, a project, a client and a meeting note (in a card opened from the board it is also on the card's panel).

## Steps

### Write a comment
1. Scroll to **Discussion** (or the comment box on the card).
2. Type into the box (it says "Add a note - @ to name someone, or say why this changed…"). Comments are written in Markdown, the same as notes.
3. Select **Comment**.

A comment can hold up to 10,000 characters. An empty one is not accepted.

### Name a colleague
1. Type **@** and the first letters of their name. A short list of matching colleagues appears.
2. Move with **↑**/**↓**, and press Enter or Tab to choose (Esc to dismiss).
3. Post as usual. Your colleague gets a **mention** in their Inbox.

Only active colleagues can be mentioned. A mention only works when the name after the **@** matches a colleague's full display name.

### Reply
1. Select **reply** under a comment. The button then says **Reply**, and a small **Replying** label appears; select **cancel** to drop it.
2. Replies are one level deep. A reply to a reply is placed under the same parent.

### Edit or delete your own
1. Under your own comment, select **edit** or **delete**. Others' comments do not show these.
2. **edit** reopens it in an editor; select **Save** (or **cancel**). An edited comment is marked "edited".
3. **delete** asks: "Delete this comment?". It leaves a marker, "This comment was deleted.", so replies still make sense. The text is gone for good.

### Read a mention
1. Open **Inbox** (the red count in the top bar includes mentions).
2. The card at the top says "Someone named you in a comment." Select the record name to go to it, which marks the mention read, or select **mark read** / **Mark all read**.

## Who can do this

Nobody needs a special permission: if you can open a record, you can read and write its discussion. If you cannot open the record, the thread does not exist for you. See [Roles and permissions](help:shell/roles-and-permissions).

## Good to know

- Comments are part of the record they hang on; they are not private messages.
- Each comment, edit and delete is logged; see [Activity and history](help:shell/activity-timeline).
- Mentions are for people typed on purpose; everything else in the Inbox is raised by the platform's rules. See [Inbox](help:insights/index).
