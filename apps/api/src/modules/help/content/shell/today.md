**Today** is where the platform opens. The address `/` simply forwards here. The heading is today's date, and below it are widgets from the modules that you choose and arrange yourself.

## What is on the page

- **The strip under the date.** Three counts, each a link to [All work](help:shell/work-hub):
  - **In progress** - cards being worked on right now.
  - **Waiting** - cards waiting on somebody outside the business, with the age in days of the oldest (for example `9d`). It turns to a warning at 7 days or more.
  - **Overdue** - cards past their due date. Zero stays quiet; only a non-zero count is highlighted.
  The strip counts cards across all projects, not only your own, and cannot be removed or rearranged. It is hidden while you are editing the layout.
- **Widgets.** Blocks such as hours logged today, your rhythm over the last fortnight, what needs you from the Inbox, and the cards in progress. Each module contributes its own; the full list is in the Reference section of Help under dashboard blocks (see [Help: search, browse and reference](help:help/index)).
- **Edit** (top right). Switches to editing mode. See [Arrange your dashboard](help:shell/arrange-dashboard).

## What you start with

Until you change anything you see a starter set: **In progress**, **Waiting on a client**, **Overdue**, **Logged today**, **Needs you**, **Your rhythm**, **Doing** and **Waiting on someone else**. Because you have not customised it, you keep getting the current starter set as it improves. The first change you make saves your own copy.

## Good to know

- **Your layout is saved on the server, per person.** It follows you to another computer or browser.
- A widget only appears if you may see what it shows. Widgets you are not allowed to use are not offered in the library at all.
- A widget that cannot say anything yet - for example a chart that needs a few finished cards - is held back from the library until there is enough data. The library tells you how many are waiting.
- If a widget has been retired since you placed it, it is quietly dropped from your page rather than showing an error.
