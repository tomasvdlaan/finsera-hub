There is nothing to configure here: the platform adapts to your device.

## Light and dark

The platform follows your device's setting. If your computer or phone is set to dark, the platform is dark; set to light, it is light. There is no switch inside the platform. Change it in your operating system or browser.

## Narrow windows

- Below roughly 1180 pixels wide, the top bar wraps: the section links move to a row of their own under the brand, clock and buttons.
- Many pages reflow to a single column on narrower windows.
- The platform has no separate phone app. On a phone you get the same pages in a narrower layout, with the same top bar.

## Full-screen pages

Meeting rooms and whiteboards fill the whole window, without the top bar, because they need the room. Use the page's own back control, or the browser's Back, to return. **⌘K** still opens the [command bar](help:shell/command-bar).

## Language

The interface is in English. The sign-in page at the identity provider is in Dutch, and a few labels elsewhere are Dutch, for example **Van** and **tot** on a colleague's page.

## Good to know

- Your dashboard layout is saved on the server, so it follows you to any device. See [Today, your dashboard](help:shell/today).
- Your recent searches in the command bar are stored in the browser, so they do not follow you to another device.
