The meeting room has a **Whiteboard** block, so you can sketch while you talk. It uses the same boards as the Whiteboards library; the meeting room just shows the one that belongs to the meeting.

## Before you start

Starting a board needs `whiteboard.write`. Anyone with `whiteboard.read` who is in the room can look at it. See [Meetings](help:meetings/index) for the room itself.

## Steps: start the meeting's board

1. In the meeting room, open the **Whiteboard** block.
2. If nothing has been drawn yet you see "Nothing has been drawn for this meeting." Click **Start a whiteboard**. (Without write access the button is not shown.)
3. The board appears in place. Draw as described in [Draw on a board](help:whiteboard/board-editor).

## Steps: use two screens

1. Click **Open full screen** at the top of the board in the room.
2. The board opens as its own page. On two screens, put it on the other one.

## Good to know

- Boards are created on request, not with every meeting, so empty boards do not clutter the library.
- The new board is called "Meeting whiteboard" and is linked to the meeting, so it also shows on the meeting's timeline. You can rename it later on its own page.
- If a meeting somehow has several boards, the room shows the one drawn on most recently.
- The toolbar and live collaboration work exactly as on a normal board. See [working together](help:whiteboard/collaboration).
- Later, the assistant can find the board by meeting and read its text, for example to answer "what did we decide about the migration?". It sees text and sticky-note labels, not drawings.
