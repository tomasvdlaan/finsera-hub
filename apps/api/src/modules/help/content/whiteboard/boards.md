The **Whiteboards** page shows every board as a card with a small picture of how it looked when somebody last left it.

## Before you start

Seeing the library needs `whiteboard.read`. The **New whiteboard** button only appears if you have `whiteboard.write`.

## Steps: start a new whiteboard

1. Open **Whiteboards** in the rail.
2. Click **New whiteboard**, top right (or on the empty page).
3. The new board opens full-screen at once, called "Untitled whiteboard". [Rename it](help:whiteboard/board-editor) when you like.

## Steps: find a board

1. Look through the cards. They are ordered by when somebody last drew on them, newest first. Boards nobody has drawn on come last.
2. Each card shows the picture, the title, and "last drawn on (date)" or "empty".
3. Click a card to open the board.

## Good to know

- The picture on the card is made when somebody who can draw leaves the board or switches away from the tab, so it shows the board as it was left, not live. A board with nothing on it has a blank tile.
- Boards started from a meeting are called "Meeting whiteboard" and also show on that meeting. See [the whiteboard in a meeting](help:whiteboard/meeting-whiteboard).
- There is no search box or filter. Titles are the way to tell boards apart, so rename new ones.
- Archived boards are not listed and there is no screen to restore or archive one.
