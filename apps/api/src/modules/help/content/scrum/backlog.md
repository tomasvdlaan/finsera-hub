The backlog is simply every card that is in no sprint. There is no separate backlog page. It is a view of the board.

## The view switch

Once a sprint is **running**, a switch appears under the sprint bar:

- **This sprint**: only the cards in the running sprint. This is the default.
- **Backlog**: cards with no sprint.
- **Everything**: all cards of the project, including done cards from finished sprints.

Without a running sprint (a flow board, or only a planned sprint) there is no switch and you see every card.

## Pull a card into the sprint

1. Choose **Backlog**.
2. Click **+ sprint** at the bottom of the card.

The card joins the running sprint and leaves the backlog view. To take a card out of a sprint, open it and set **Sprint** to **Backlog** ([Open and edit a card](help:scrum/edit-a-card)). You can also set the sprint there for a planned sprint.

## Planning a sprint that has not started

The sprint bar says "Put cards in it from the backlog first — starting is what makes it the board." Before it starts, put cards in using the **Sprint** field in each card, because **+ sprint** only works for the running sprint. See [Plan and run a sprint](help:scrum/plan-and-run-a-sprint).

## Good to know

- Adding a card to a sprint, or taking one out, while the sprint is **running** is recorded as a scope change. It shows later in [Sprint history](help:scrum/sprint-history) as "N arrived after it started".
- Cards you create while **This sprint** is selected go into the sprint. See [Add a card](help:scrum/add-a-card).
- A sprint's finished cards keep their sprint, so after it closes they show under **Everything**, not **Backlog**.
- When a sprint is finished, its unfinished cards fall back into the backlog on their own.
- The assistant can put a card into a sprint or out of one.
