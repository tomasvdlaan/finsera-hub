The **Activity** tab of [a client's page](help:crm/client-profile) answers "what was said, what is it connected to, and what happened". It has three panels.

## Discussion

A comment thread on the client.

1. Type your note in the box at the bottom. Typing `@` offers a list of colleagues to mention.
2. Post it. You can reply to a comment, and edit or delete your own (deleting asks for confirmation).

## Timeline

What has happened to the client, assembled by the platform from its records, links and events, including activity on linked items. A change of stage is one example. It is read-only.

## Links

Links connect the client to other clients and projects (the picker offers every client and project).

1. Under **Links**, choose the target in the **Link to…** drop-down.
2. Choose the kind: **about**, **discussed**, **originated_from** or **relates_to**.
3. Click **Link**.

Existing links are listed with their kind. Click **remove** to take one off.

## Good to know

- Links that are part of the structure (a project belonging to its client) cannot be removed, so they show no **remove** button.
- Changing the stage on the client's page refreshes the timeline straight away.
- The same three panels exist at the bottom of [a project's page](help:crm/project-detail).
